# Programmable biopolymers

> Leverage biomacromolecules (nucleotides, amino acids, sugars, lipids) into more programmable, new-to-nature composite polymers, including through non-canonical amino acids.

## Linked ideas

- *extends* → [[Classifying materials]]: If DNA is just a programmable polymer, the practical question is which other biomacromolecules can be made as cheap to synthesise, assemble and sequence, and combined into new composite polymers.
- *enables* → [[Scaffolds, nanoparticles & adhesives]]: Mussel-inspired peptide wet-adhesives built with non-canonical residues could replace cyanoacrylate in surgery, a concrete payoff for expanding the genetic code.
- *echoes* → [[A shared language]]: The same gap at the molecular scale: no common vocabulary between the biologists who build the tools, the chemists who would formulate with them and the regulators who would certify them.
