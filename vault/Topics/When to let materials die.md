# When to let materials die

> Which living functions justify ongoing moisture, nutrients and care—and when should we let materials die after growing them?

## Linked ideas

- *echoes* → [[Living vs formerly living]] — Whether a material counts as living has practical consequences: a living material needs ongoing moisture, nutrients and care, and someone has to decide when to stop providing them.
- *extends* → [[Survive use, degrade after]] — If a material is designed to live, the choice to stop caring for it is the end-of-life trigger.
- *echoes* → [[Duties to organisms]] — If a living material is alive, what do we owe it? Ongoing care — or the decision to let it die — is an ethical question as well as a technical one.
