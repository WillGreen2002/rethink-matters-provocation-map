## Linked ideas

- *echoes* → [[Signalling & crosstalk]] — Same trade-off at different scales — how robust is robust, and how robust is too robust? Tissue signalling and bioreactor feedstocks both ask when to tolerate variation and when to shield against it.
- *enables* → [[Waste valorisation]] — Valorising waste only works if bioprocesses can cope with the variable, inconsistent inputs that waste streams are.
- *echoes* → [[Printing meets growth]] — Manufacturing expects uniform inputs and outputs, while biological materials often get their properties from randomness. Both topics ask whether variability is a defect to remove or a resource to design with.
