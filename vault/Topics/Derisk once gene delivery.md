# Derisk once: gene delivery

> Should a living therapeutic material platform be derisked once for gene delivery and reapplied for other diseases?

**Theme:** [[Theme 5 · Medical & long-term safety]]  
**Scale:** macro · **Working group:** WG2 (proposed) · **Pillar:** governance, translation · **Value:** equity

## Linked ideas

- *extends* → [[Funding platform medicines]]: If a platform can be derisked once, the funding question changes: the cost of proving safety is shared across every disease it later serves.
- *extends* → [[Derisk once synthesis platform]]: The same idea one level up: derisk the whole synthesis platform, not just the delivery vehicle.
- *echoes* → [[Standardising dynamic properties]]: Derisk-once logic and standardisation are the same bet: a validated, reusable platform or property set that does not have to be re-proved every time.
