# Printing meets growth

## Linked ideas

- *enables* → [[World models & inverse design]] — World models would predict how multicellular systems evolve under imposed constraints, turning 'design the initial conditions' into inverse design.
- *extends* → [[Fabrication readiness]] — Guided morphogenesis moves fabrication from static shaping to setting initial conditions; whether today's methods are ready for that is the next question.
- *echoes* → [[Material 'intelligence']] — When does guided morphogenesis stop being 'structure' and start behaving like a primitive living, intelligent material? The same question sits at the end of printing plus self-assembly.
- *echoes* → [[Variable feedstocks]] — Manufacturing expects uniform inputs and outputs, while biological materials often get their properties from randomness. Both topics ask whether variability is a defect to remove or a resource to design with.
- *tension* → [[Portable design principles]] — Self-assembly supplies hierarchy and emergence; machines supply control. Extracting a principle rather than copying a structure points towards designing initial conditions and constraints instead of final form.
