# Signalling & crosstalk

> What about controlling signalling, preventing unwanted crosstalk and balancing robustness with adaptability?

**Theme:** [[Theme 4 · Programming & biofabrication]]  
**Scale:** meso · **Working group:** WG1 · **Pillar:** design · **Value:** humility

## Discussions behind this

- [[Shielding, crosstalk and how robust is too robust]] (RM009)

## Linked ideas

- *echoes* → [[Biosensor design]]: Engineering a signal and shielding against unwanted crosstalk are two halves of signalling control.
- *echoes* → [[Variable feedstocks]]: Same trade-off at different scales: how robust is robust, and how robust is too robust? Tissue signalling and bioreactor feedstocks both ask when to tolerate variation and when to shield against it.
- *echoes* → [[Survive use, degrade after]]: Robust enough to survive use, adaptable enough to give way afterwards: the same design tension at the scale of a product's whole life.
- *enables* → [[Patterning to organs]]: Spatial pattern is built out of signals; whether a pattern holds depends on how well one signal is insulated from another.
