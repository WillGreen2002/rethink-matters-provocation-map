# Follow the value — and the cost

Who pays, who owns and who benefits? These notes ask the same distributive question about waste chains, manufacturing, medicines and intellectual property.

1. [[Full cost of waste]] — Waste feedstocks look cheap until collection, transport, storage, preprocessing and contamination are counted.
2. [[Who captures value]] — Who captures the value across a waste-to-material chain — and who carries the cost?
3. [[Energy, cost & fit]] — Energy requirements, cost and infrastructure decide which materials can reach scale.
4. [[Funding platform medicines]] — Where no market exists, who funds platform medicines and the regulatory path they need?
5. [[IP & biological design]] — Ownership of genes and designs shapes who ultimately benefits.
6. [[Care & convivial tech]] — Convivial technology treats access — who can make, use and repair a thing — as a design requirement.

**So what for the central question:** A world worth living in distributes benefits and burdens deliberately; these notes show where the distribution is currently left to chance.
