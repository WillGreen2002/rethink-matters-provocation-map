# Words before methods

Biologists, materials scientists, engineers, designers and regulators describe similar phenomena in different languages. Several themes begin with definitions because nothing else can be compared until they agree.

1. [[Biobased vs biomaterial]] — One word, three meanings: function, interaction, origin.
2. [[Classifying materials]] — Classify by application, composition, properties or level of biological control?
3. [[A shared language]] — A portable vocabulary across biology, materials science, engineering and design.
4. [[Non-canonical amino acids]] — At the molecular scale there is no common vocabulary between those who build biological tools, those who formulate with them and those who must certify them.
5. [[Standardising dynamic properties]] — Standardising sensing, repair and adaptation means agreeing which parameters are shared.
6. [[Public datasets]] — …which a public dataset can then record, so that results become comparable.

**So what for the central question:** Shared language is the quiet infrastructure of every other conversation on this map.
