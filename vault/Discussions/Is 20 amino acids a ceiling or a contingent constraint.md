# Is 20 amino acids a ceiling or a contingent constraint?

*Proposed*

## Provocation

> Biology's industrial chemistry is constrained by what 20 canonical amino acids can catalyse, and this is treated as a ceiling rather than a contingent constraint. Expanding the genetic code with non-canonical amino acids opens material classes and chemical space physically inaccessible to either incumbent synthesis or natural biology, but no shared vocabulary exists between the molecular biologists who design the biological tools, the chemical/protein engineers who would formulate around them, and the regulators who would need to certify them. The most consequential biomaterials of the next decade may be made up of polymers and adhesives whose monomers contain ncAAs biology has never used (i.e peptide wet-adhesives themed on mussel foot proteins with ncAA DOPA residues to replace cyanoacrylate in surgery glues), and we will not get there with single-discipline thinking.
