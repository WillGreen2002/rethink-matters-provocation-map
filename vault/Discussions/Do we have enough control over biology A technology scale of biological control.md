# Do we have enough control over biology? A technology scale of biological control

*RM008 (2026-08-11)* · notes RM008.5

## Provocation

> Do we have enough control over biology at the moment for biomaterials, rather than just optimising production of an output that already exists: biomaterials differ from recombinant technologies as spatial organisation is important. We can mimic cellular niches for regenerative medicine to create organoids but not generate full sized vascularised organs as the spatial pattern of coordinated tissue growth is beyond 1 dimensional growth factor applications. Therefore can we use this input control theory to categorise biomaterials; Can we organise our idealistic biomaterial on to a technology scale of biological control. Will this change our focus or help us acknowledge what is market ready or deep research.
