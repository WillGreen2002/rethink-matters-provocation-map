# Is the future of biology materials? Biofabrication as a method

*RM005 (2026-05-18)* · notes RM005.8

## Provocation

> Is the future of biology materials? Assessing biofabrication as fabrication method. For e.g. What will be best method to produce ordered biological materials from anisotropically aligned biopolymers (polysaccharides and proteins specifically) at the nanoscale? Possible approaches: in vivo or in vitro self-assembly, microfluidic shear alignment ... or something else entirely? Taking inspiration from nanomaterials like chiral nematic liquid crystals templates that are used to align nanoparticles
