# Does classing biomaterials by application help or hinder?

*Proposed*

## Provocation

> Does classing biomaterials by application help or hinder thinking:
> 
> Group 1 biomaterial: non engineered cultured cells for medical tissue applications
> 
> Group 1B biomaterials: Engineered cultured cells for medical tissue applications
> 
> Group 2 biomaterial: cellular or Abiotic scaffolds and niche interfaces for Group 1 biomaterials (printed architectures)
> 
> Group 3 Biomaterials: engineered Group 2 for bioprocess
> 
> Group 4 Biomaterials: Bulk materials production form recombinant means
> 
> Group 5 biomaterials: plant lignocellulosic materials.
