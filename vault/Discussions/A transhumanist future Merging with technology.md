# A transhumanist future? Merging with technology

*RM005 (2026-05-18)* · notes RM005.6

## Provocation

> Is human nature bound to converge to a transhumanist future? Is our species' survival only possible through further merging with technology, e.g. using brain implants, nanotransducers, exoskeletons, to be able to survive future circumstances with super intelligent AI entities? What are the possible implications of such a future for public safety, security and morality?

## Post-meeting synthesis (anonymised digest)

- The discussion started from early clinical-trial results for a brain implant on the motor cortex (for example, controlling a wheelchair by thought).
- Where might this go? The most influential players seem most interested in enhancement, to keep up with AI (brain–AI interfaces).
- Which regulations would protect consumers if an implant company goes bankrupt (real cases exist where users and physicians lost access to the proprietary software needed to recalibrate a device), or protect agency, for example against hacking?
- Might biomaterials and biological modification turn out to be a more likely route to human enhancement than electronics, or a mixture of both?
- What are the benefits? Would people be left with no real choice, as has happened with smartphones?
