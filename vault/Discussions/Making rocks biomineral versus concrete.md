# Making rocks: biomineral versus concrete

*RM004 (2026-04-25)* · notes RM004.4

## Provocation

> Talking about making rocks. Will it be as good as modern concrete? what about the prize competition of natural carbonates vs biomineral one? what biomineral can give carbonates industry doesnt have yet?

## Post-meeting synthesis (anonymised digest)

- Removing the need for a carbonation step would be a major win — ideally coupling calcium carbonate directly with other elements to mimic Portland cement.
- Alternatives already exist: supplementary cementitious materials (fly ash, slag, silica fume) partly replace Portland cement, cutting CO₂ and cost while improving durability.
- Biomineralisation can reduce CO₂ emissions and some companies already pursue it (for example [Biomason](https://biomason.com/) and [BioZeroC](https://www.biozeroc.com/)).
- Of interest beyond carbonate: control over crystal shape, microstructure, polymorphs and hierarchical structuring; plus added functionality such as self-healing and integration with living systems.
