# “We don’t need more solutions”

*RM007 (2026-07-11)*

## Provocation

> We dont need more solutions. for most problems there are already multiple adequate mitigations. the issue is the petulant unwillingness to implement them because everything comes with drawbacks and societal inertia prefers the current drawbacks to novel ones.

## Post-meeting synthesis (anonymised digest)

- Perception of risk and social inertia: risk mitigation brings costs, and the perceived cost is a drop in quality of life.
- It is also geopolitical: everyone would need to slow down at the same time.
- Ideas: innovation funds that give individuals and small or medium companies room to explore; more public engagement; incentives for repair, reuse and slowing down built in (reuse–repair–recycle).
