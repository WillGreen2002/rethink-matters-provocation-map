# What if gradients were the core design notion?

*RM008 (2026-08-11)* · notes RM008.4

## Provocation

> Mark Miodownik has written about: solids, liquids, and gases. What I sense is missing from his books however is exploring how these synergistically interact with one another in the body. For example: is a cell solid? Perhaps it is the sub-type of cell (and its physical microenvironment) that defines whether it is solid or liquid i.e. a bone cell is 'solid' but a gut cell may be less so?
> 
> *What I'm trying to get at is that biology has gradients; diffusion gradients in liquids, solid gradients in bone porosity, heat gradients in the convection in a room... . What if we designed biomaterials with a gradient as the core design notion?
