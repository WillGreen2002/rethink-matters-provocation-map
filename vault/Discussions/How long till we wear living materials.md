# How long till we wear living materials?

*RM008 (2026-08-11)* · notes RM008.2

## Provocation

> How long till we will wear living materials?
> 
> In the broader sense, is a living material something that interacts with its surrounding?
> 
> If the material was once alive but has now died, is it still considered a living material? E.g. a microbial textile may have been halted in growth but would be considered a living material. Likewise however, a cotton shirt was also once living and growing in a field...
