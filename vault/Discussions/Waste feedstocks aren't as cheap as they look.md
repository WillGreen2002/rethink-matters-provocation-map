# Waste feedstocks aren’t as “cheap” as they look

*Proposed*

## Provocation

> Waste feedstocks are often framed as “cheap” inputs, but their unit economics can be undermined by collection, transportation, storage, preprocessing, contamination, and variability. The real question is whether the costs of securing, standardising, and converting waste streams are outweighed by their value in biobased materials - and who captures that value across the supply chain.
