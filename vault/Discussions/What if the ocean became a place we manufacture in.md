# What if the ocean became a place we manufacture in?

*RM008 (2026-08-11)* · notes RM008.1

## Provocation

> Open to speculation: What if the ocean becomes a place we manufacture in? Manipulating pressure, salinity, currents, and marine organisms as inputs to entirely new material processes?
> 
> Imagine coral-like structures grown on the seabed, pelagic material farms drifting through currents, or materials that can only be made at depth.
> 
> What if wetsuits, dive masks, and hulls evolved into living interfaces: self-healing skins, hydrogel eyes, artificial gills, or synthetic sharkskin surfaces?
> 
> What materials, manufacturing technologies, and new relationships with marine ecosystems would we need to make that future possible?
