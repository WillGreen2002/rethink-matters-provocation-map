# Theme 7. Circularity, waste and adaptive manufacturing

> What would make a circular bioeconomy technically and economically workable?

Part of [[00 A world we want to live in|A world we want to live in]] · chapter: *How technology becomes society* · theme 5 in the Bristol retreat sheet

## A future we'd want

A future where waste, protein-based waste included, feeds bioprocesses that tolerate variable feedstocks, the full cost of waste is counted, and who captures value, who carries cost and whether new materials complement or complicate reuse are worked out openly.
