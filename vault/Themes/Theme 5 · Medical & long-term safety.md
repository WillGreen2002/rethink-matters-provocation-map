# Theme 5. Medical applications and long-term safety

> Can biomaterials deliver reliable clinical benefits over meaningful timescales?

Part of [[00 A world we want to live in|A world we want to live in]] · chapter: *How life becomes technology* · theme 7 in the Bristol retreat sheet

## A future we'd want

A future where medical biomaterials rest on long-term evidence of degradation, toxicity and tissue response, dressing signals are reliable enough to read, and the role of clinical judgement and the funding of genetic medicines without a market are decided openly.
