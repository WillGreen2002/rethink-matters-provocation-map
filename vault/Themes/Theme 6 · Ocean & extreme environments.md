# Theme 6. Ocean futures and extreme environments

> Could biomaterials help us live, manufacture and adapt underwater, in space and amid climate extremes, and what maintenance would they require?

Part of [[00 A world we want to live in|A world we want to live in]] · chapter: *How life becomes technology* · theme 9 in the Bristol retreat sheet

## A future we'd want

A future where underwater habitation must justify itself beyond research and niche industry; materials that survive extremes are planned to degrade, by trigger or collection, though control is limited; and which living functions justify ongoing care is decided openly.
