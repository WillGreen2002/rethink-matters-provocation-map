# Theme 9. Ethics, ownership and social purpose

> What would make biomaterials ethically and socially better?

Part of [[00 A world we want to live in|A world we want to live in]] · chapter: *How technology becomes society* · theme 8 in the Bristol retreat sheet

## A future we'd want

A future where biological origin is not mistaken for goodness, shared tools come with the means to use them safely, people keep control of implanted devices, and duties to organisms and ownership of genes and designs are weighed openly.
