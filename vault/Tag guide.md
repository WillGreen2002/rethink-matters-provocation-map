# Tag guide

Two layers: **lenses** (every topic carries them) and **semantic tags** (the reasons two notes belong together).

## Lenses

### Scale (`scale/…`) — At what length scale does the problem become visible?

The group's own Micro / Meso / Macro format (sign-up sheet 'Read me').

- `scale/micro` **Micro** — Molecular structure, material properties, surface chemistry, biological functionality.
- `scale/meso` **Meso** — Structure and interfaces, processing, reproducibility, measurement gaps, human perception, inter/intraspecies ecological interactions.
- `scale/macro` **Macro** — Scale-up manufacturing, regulation, cost, adoption, socio-ethical issues, infrastructure, regenerative ecology.

### Working group (`wg/…`) — Which working group would carry this forward?

The 'Relevant working group' column of the sign-up sheets. Where a session recorded no group, the map proposes one (shown dashed).

- `wg/wg1` **WG1 · Prediction, knowledge & metrics** — Models, data, definitions and the metrics needed to know what a biomaterial does.
- `wg/wg2` **WG2 · Utopian to utilisation & socioeconomic application** — From speculative aims to real use: economics, society, ethics, adoption.
- `wg/wg3` **WG3 · Manufacturing & infrastructure** — How things are made at scale, and the infrastructure, supply chains and environments they need.

### Pillar (`pillar/…`) — Which part of the Rethink Matters mission does it advance?

The tagline on rethinkmatters.org: 'climate performance, biomimetic design, and biological governance for translation'. Colours follow the tagline.

- `pillar/design` **Biomimetic design** — Learning from, and designing with, biological principles, structures and processes — including the tools (prediction, programming) that make that possible.
- `pillar/climate` **Climate performance** — Environmental performance of biomaterials: carbon, energy, circularity, waste, degradation, living and manufacturing in climate extremes.
- `pillar/governance` **Biological governance** — Safety, ethics, regulation, ownership and responsibility for engineered biology.
- `pillar/translation` **Translation** — Getting from lab to use: scale-up, economics, adoption, standards, evidence and infrastructure.

### Value (`value/…`) — Which value does it speak to in 'a world in which we want to live'?

The four values the World Economic Forum report uses as its lens (equity, humility, sustainability, solidarity). Assigned by the map; not every note carries one.

- `value/equity` **Equity** — Fair access to benefits, costs and risks; who funds, owns and decides.
- `value/humility` **Humility** — Acknowledging uncertainty and limits; evidence before hype; learning from nature and from failure.
- `value/sustainability` **Sustainability** — Planetary and long-term performance: circularity, energy, durability, care.
- `value/solidarity` **Solidarity** — Shared language, shared infrastructure, open data, collective responsibility and care.
